  <p>
    Building a digital ecosystem designed to make motorcycle services more organized, traceable, and accessible.

  <img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/PROJECT-PRIVATE-0D47A1?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white" alt="Private project" />

### What I'm building

- **RODEX ID:** Unique motorcycle identification through QR codes.
- **Digital service history:** Structured records of motorcycle maintenance.
- **Customer management:** Organized customer and motorcycle information.
- **Work orders:** Digital service operations and maintenance workflows.
- **Inventory management:** Product tracking and stock control.
- **Business operations:** Digital tools to connect and organize service processes.

> RODEX is a private project under development. The platform combines software, business operations, and motorcycle maintenance into one ecosystem.
